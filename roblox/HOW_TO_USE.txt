SUBJECT ZERO - Roblox version (Chapter 1 map)
=============================================

1. Install Roblox Studio (free): create.roblox.com -> "Start Creating".
2. Open Roblox Studio, sign in.
3. File -> Open from File... -> pick  SubjectZero_Chapter1.rbxlx
   (it takes a little while: it's a big map).
4. Press Play (or F5). You start in the WAITING ROOM.
   PICK A CHARACTER, the easy way: click one of the 4 big buttons at
   the bottom of the screen. (Or walk onto a stand, or press E at it.)
   Each player a different one; click another free one to change.
   You stay as your own avatar.
   The board on the wall shows who picked what. When EVERYONE has picked
   (2-4 players), a 10 second countdown starts and the whole team goes
   into the game together. (Testing alone in Studio works too.)
   Someone who joins later picks a free character and goes straight in.
   The characters:
     THE SON (Ethan) ... SENSE [Q]: see the Long Man and useful things
                         through walls for 4 seconds.
     THE JOURNALIST .... CAMERA FLASH [Q]: point it at the Long Man to
                         blind him for 3 seconds.
     THE ENGINEER ...... FIXER: repairs the generator and puts in fuses
                         3x faster.
     THE GUARD (Frank) . STRONG: moves the heavy cabinet alone.
                         TOUGH: once per life, shoves the Long Man off.
                         A bit slower.
   (On a controller the ability button is B / Circle. On a phone, tap
   the ability button on the screen.)
   Then the OPENING CUTSCENE plays (Space / A to skip), and you're on
   the road as YOUR OWN AVATAR, in FIRST PERSON, with
   your character's name over your head.
   - Your FLASHLIGHT is in your inventory (the bar at the bottom).
     Press 1 (or F, Y on Xbox, Triangle on PlayStation) to take it out:
     it turns on. Click (or R2) to switch it off and on.
   - Shift (keyboard), left stick click (controller): SPRINT (about 4 seconds)
   - E (keyboard), X (Xbox), Square (PlayStation): use things / hold things
   - Walk into an item to pick it up (you don't have to hold it to use it).

THE LOBBY (so people can find each other and play together)
-----------------------------------------------------------
There are TWO files:
  SubjectZero_Lobby.rbxlx ..... the LOBBY: a big hall where everyone
                                arrives, with 4 ELEVATORS.
  SubjectZero_Chapter1.rbxlx .. the GAME.
The lobby is an abandoned Site 12 visitor hall: flickering lights, blood,
claw marks... and an OBSERVATION WINDOW. Something in there watches you.
Sometimes it slams against the glass.
In the lobby, walk into an elevator with your friends (or with anyone!).
When 2-4 players are in it, it counts down 15 seconds and takes that group
to their OWN private game. The first person in an elevator can press
"Make it friends only". There's an "Invite friends" button too.
After the ending, everyone comes back to the lobby.

Set it up once (both files go into ONE Roblox game):
 1. Open SubjectZero_Lobby.rbxlx in Studio.
    File -> Publish to Roblox -> "Create new game" -> give it a name.
    (The lobby is now the place people arrive in.)
 2. Still in the lobby: View -> Asset Manager -> Places.
    Right-click in the empty space -> "Add New Place".
 3. Open SubjectZero_Chapter1.rbxlx.
    File -> Publish to Roblox As... -> pick YOUR game -> pick the new
    place (not the lobby!) -> Publish / Overwrite.
 4. That's it: the elevators find the game place by themselves.
    Game Settings (in the lobby) -> Places: set the lobby's server size
    to 20 or 30, and Chapter 1's server size to 4.
 5. To test: play it from the Roblox website or app (not Studio: Roblox
    only moves players between places in the real game). Ask a friend
    to join, or open a second Roblox window.
(In Studio you can still test each file on its own.)

To let friends play (PC, phone, Xbox, PlayStation):
5. File -> Publish to Roblox -> give it a name.
6. Home -> Game Settings:
   - Places: Server size / "Max players" = 4 (it's a 4-player co-op game)
   - Avatar: leave "Player choice" ON so everyone keeps their own avatar
   - Permissions: Public (or Friends) when you're ready
   - For Xbox / PlayStation, see "XBOX AND PS5" below.

XBOX AND PS5
------------
The game already works with a controller:
   Left stick ........ walk          Right stick ... look around
   A / Cross ......... jump          L3 (click left stick) ... sprint
   X / Square ........ use / hold things (gate, switches, fuses, keys...)
   Y / Triangle ...... take out the flashlight / switch it on and off
   R2 ................ switch the flashlight off and on
   D-pad UP .......... use the keypad
   On the keypad and notes: move with the stick, A / Cross to press,
   B / Circle to close. Text is bigger on a TV.

To put it ON Xbox and PlayStation (after "Publish to Roblox"):
 a. Studio: Home -> Game Settings -> Basic Info -> "Playable Devices":
    tick  Console  (keep Computer, Phone and Tablet ticked too). Save.
 b. Go to create.roblox.com -> Creations -> your game ->
    "Experience Questionnaire" (also called Maturity & Compliance).
    Answer it honestly (it's a horror game: say yes to "scary" /
    "fear" content). Consoles only show games that have this filled in.
    It will probably be rated "Mild" or "Moderate", so very young players
    won't see it.
 c. Make the game Public (Game Settings -> Permissions).
 d. On the Xbox / PS5: open the Roblox app and search for your game's name.
    (Friends on PC, phone, Xbox and PS5 can all play together.)
Test it with a controller first: plug an Xbox or PS controller into your
PC and press Play in Studio. Or: Test tab -> Device -> pick "Xbox One"
to see how the screen looks on a TV.

What's in this Roblox version:
- The opening cutscene, then first person (like the PC game)
- The four characters with their own abilities
- A new outside: real terrain (grass, a dirt yard, a cracked path, the
  road with muddy edges, rocks, rolling hills), pine trees, drifting
  ground mist, and a misty moonlit sky with stars
  (the terrain appears when you press Play)
- An old 1960s lab underground: green and cream tiled walls, checkered
  floors, lab benches with specimen jars, glowing growth tanks, old
  reel-to-reel computers, a gurney, pipes, warning signs, and a padded cell
- The whole Chapter 1 map (forest, station, stairs, tunnels, cell, elevator room)
- Night, fog, flickering and pulsing lights
- Your own avatar with a flashlight
- THE LONG MAN (Subject 7), crawling on all fours:
  * He waits in his cell in the tunnels. Walk past it and everyone sees
    the "SUBJECT 7" reveal... then he hunts.
  * He crawls along his route and finds his way around walls.
  * When he SEES you he stops, tilts his head and stares... then charges.
    Your flashlight ON makes him see you from further away!
  * Lights and flashlights flicker when he is close.
  * If he catches you: JUMP SCARE... and the whole team dies with you!
    Then he crawls away for a few seconds.
  * To test fast: in Studio press Play, then walk to the tunnels
    (down the hidden stairs) and past his cell.
  * When he STARES at you, he slowly RISES UP to his full 9 feet, his
    head tipping over sideways, his jaw falling open. Then he charges,
    in sudden lunges.
  * His red eyes glow in the dark. Lights go OUT when he's right there.
  * When he's hunting you: the screen darkens and goes red at the edges,
    your view shakes, and you hear your heartbeat speeding up.

HIS SOUNDS (do this once, it makes him MUCH scarier)
Roblox only plays sounds that are uploaded to Roblox, so:
 1. Publish the game first (File -> Publish to Roblox).
 2. View -> Asset Manager -> Import (the up-arrow button). Pick all 7
    files from the "sounds" folder in this zip. Wait for them to upload
    (Roblox checks them first; it can take a few minutes).
 3. In the Asset Manager, open Audio. DRAG each sound onto
    ReplicatedStorage -> LongManSounds in the Explorer.
    That's it: the game finds them by their names (LongMan_Scream,
    LongMan_Breath, ...).
    If dragging doesn't work: RIGHT-CLICK each sound -> Insert. It goes
    into Workspace, and the game finds it there too (keep its name).
 4. File -> Publish to Roblox again.
(Or: paste a sound's ID, like rbxassetid://123456, into the Value of the
matching item in LongManSounds. Both ways work.)
(The game works without them too, just silent.)

  * Want him faster or slower? Open Workspace > LongMan > LongManBrain
    and change CHASE_SPEED (17). Walking is 14, sprinting is 22.
- THE TEAM LIVES OR DIES TOGETHER: if ONE player dies, EVERYONE dies,
  and the whole team comes back at the last checkpoint (the yard, the
  tunnels, or the elevator room). Puzzles you solved stay solved.
- THE PUZZLES (the same as the PC game). Each one needs 2 people:
  1. The gate: one person LIFTS it and holds it (stay still!). Another
     finds the CROWBAR by the shed and jams the gate open.
  2. The door code: put the WELCOME TAPE in the tape machine, and someone
     HOLDS the monitor switch by the office door. The screen in the west
     office shows the code. Shout it to the person at the keypad
     (at the keypad press R, or D-pad UP on a controller).
  3. The GREEN KEYCARD is in the director's office. The filing cabinet
     hides a door: TWO people push it at the same time.
  4. Tunnels: one person holds the VENT cover up, the others crawl in
     (you shrink to fit!) and slide open the bolt on the other side.
  5. Elevator room: find 2 FUSES in the tunnels and put them in the fuse
     boxes, repair the GENERATOR, read the CONTAINMENT ORDER on the desk,
     then turn both LAUNCH KEYS at the same time ("3, 2, 1, now!").
  6. Everyone gets in the elevator. Press Descend... END OF CHAPTER 1.
  The door code is new every time you play.
