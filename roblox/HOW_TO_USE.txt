SUBJECT ZERO - Roblox version (Chapter 1 map)
=============================================

1. Install Roblox Studio (free): create.roblox.com -> "Start Creating".
2. Open Roblox Studio, sign in.
3. File -> Open from File... -> pick  SubjectZero_Chapter1.rbxlx
   (it takes a little while: it's a big map).
4. Press Play (or F5). You spawn on the road as YOUR OWN AVATAR.
   - F (keyboard), Y (Xbox), Triangle (PlayStation): flashlight on/off
   - Mouse wheel / right stick: zoom in to first person, or out to see your avatar
   - Shift (keyboard), left stick click (controller): SPRINT (about 4 seconds)
   - E (keyboard), X (Xbox), Square (PlayStation): use things / hold things
   - Walk into an item to pick it up. 1-9 or click it in the bar to hold it.

To let friends play (PC, phone, Xbox, PlayStation):
5. File -> Publish to Roblox -> give it a name.
6. Home -> Game Settings:
   - Places: Server size / "Max players" = 4 (it's a 4-player co-op game)
   - Avatar: leave "Player choice" ON so everyone keeps their own avatar
   - Permissions: Public (or Friends) when you're ready
   - To be on Xbox/PlayStation the experience needs an age/content
     maturity questionnaire filled in (Roblox asks for it in Game Settings).

What's in this first Roblox version:
- The whole Chapter 1 map (forest, station, stairs, tunnels, cell, elevator room)
- Night, fog, flickering and pulsing lights
- Your own avatar with a flashlight
- THE LONG MAN (Subject 7), crawling on all fours:
  * He waits in his cell in the tunnels. Walk past it and everyone sees
    the "SUBJECT 7" reveal... then he hunts.
  * He crawls along his route and finds his way around walls.
  * When he SEES you he stops, tilts his head and stares... then charges.
    Your flashlight ON makes him see you from further away!
  * Lights and flashlights flicker when he is close.
  * If he catches you: JUMP SCARE, and you respawn at the start.
    Then he crawls away for a few seconds.
  * To test fast: in Studio press Play, then walk to the tunnels
    (down the hidden stairs) and past his cell.
  * Want a scream sound? Upload a sound to Roblox (Creator Hub),
    then in Studio click StarterPlayer > StarterPlayerScripts >
    LongManEffects, and add an Attribute named ScreamSoundId (string)
    with the value  rbxassetid://YOUR_NUMBER
  * Want him faster or slower? Open Workspace > LongMan > LongManBrain
    and change CHASE_SPEED (17). Walking is 14, sprinting is 22.
- THE PUZZLES (the same as the PC game). Each one needs 2 people:
  1. The gate: one person LIFTS it and holds it (stay still!). Another
     finds the CROWBAR by the shed and jams the gate open.
  2. The door code: put the WELCOME TAPE in the tape machine, and someone
     HOLDS the monitor switch by the office door. The screen in the west
     office shows the code. Shout it to the person at the keypad
     (press R at the keypad).
  3. The GREEN KEYCARD is in the director's office. The filing cabinet
     hides a door: TWO people push it at the same time.
  4. Tunnels: one person holds the VENT cover up, the others crawl in
     (you shrink to fit!) and slide open the bolt on the other side.
  5. Elevator room: find 2 FUSES in the tunnels and put them in the fuse
     boxes, repair the GENERATOR, read the CONTAINMENT ORDER on the desk,
     then turn both LAUNCH KEYS at the same time ("3, 2, 1, now!").
  6. Everyone gets in the elevator. Press Descend... END OF CHAPTER 1.
  The door code is new every time you play.
