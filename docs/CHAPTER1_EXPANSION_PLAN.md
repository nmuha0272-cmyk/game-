### NEW ADDITIONS:

#### **1. Perimeter Patrol Route Puzzle (3-4 minutes)**
**Setup:**

- Team must find the **breaker box** in the supply shed to disable sensors

**Teamwork:**
- One player finds keycard
- One player unlocks shed
- Engineer reads breaker diagram, tells teammate which switches to flip
- Others keep watch (lights flash = almost triggered)

**Reward:** Alarm stays off, team learns about working together under pressure

## ROOM 4A: SECURITY CHECKPOINT — NEW ROOM (+6-10 minutes)

### **Location:**
Between the office stairwell and the maintenance tunnels. A decontamination checkpoint with sealed doors.

### **Layout:**
- **Entry Airlock:** Metal detector arch (inactive), x-ray machine
- **Security Office:** Behind glass, CCTV monitors (all static), logbook
- **Holding Cell:** Small room with bench, where "uncleared" visitors waited
- **Weapon Locker:** Behind security glass (contains Chapter 1's weapons)

### **The Puzzle: Decontamination Sequence**

**Problem:** The tunnel-side door is sealed. The console reads:  
*"DECON SEQUENCE INCOMPLETE. RESTART PROTOCOL."*

**Solution (3 players minimum):**

1. **Guard** → Finds **Yellow Keycard** in security desk drawer
2. **Engineer** → Uses keycard to access decon control panel
3. **Panel shows:** *"UV CYCLE: OFFLINE. VENT SEAL: OPEN. PRESSURE: LOW."*
4. **Three simultaneous actions needed:**
   - **Player 1** (any): Enter holding cell, close vent manually (wheel crank, 8 seconds)
   - **Player 2** (Engineer preferred): Restore UV lights (flip breaker, hold 5 seconds)
   - **Player 3** (any): Hold pressure valve lever at airlock door

**If all three hold their positions for 5 seconds:** Door unlocks

**If anyone lets go early:** System resets, must start over

### **Tension Element:**
- **Static CCTV monitors suddenly flicker to LIVE**
- One shows the tunnels ahead... something tall moves across the screen
- **First Long Man sighting** (on screen only, not in person yet)
- Players realize they're about to enter his territory

### **Loot (inside weapon locker after door opens):**
- .38 Revolver (6 rounds loaded)
- 9mm Pistol (empty, mag on floor)
- Box of 12 loose 9mm rounds
- 2 speedloaders for revolver
- Shotgun shells (4 only)
- **Note on locker:** "In case of outbreak: SHOOT CENTER MASS. DO NOT APPROACH DOWNED SUBJECTS."

**Time added:** 6-10 minutes (puzzle + exploration + tension building)

## ROOM 4B: TUNNELS — EXPANSION (+5 minutes)

### Current:
- Vent crawl
- Power room
- Darkroom
- Flood drain
- Long Man chase

### NEW ADDITIONS:

#### **1. Second Vent Path — "The Bypass" (2-3 minutes)**
**Problem:** Power room door is sealed from inside  
**Current solution:** Vent crawl from alcove  
**New layer:** The first vent is **blocked** (cave-in)

**New Solution:**
- Find **alternative vent entrance** in the flooded area (below water level)
- One player must wade through flood with **gas mask** (toxic gas pocket near vent)
- Crawl through tight space while teammate guides via walkie-talkie
- **Walkie-talkie use attracts Long Man** → other players must distract him

**Adds:** Gas mask use, walkie noise discipline, split-team coordination

---

#### **2. Extended Chase Sequence (3-4 minutes)**
**Current:** One chase through tunnels  
**New:** **Multi-stage chase** with phases:

**Phase 1: Detection**
- Long Man spots team in power room
- Lights flicker, his footsteps echo
- Team has 5 seconds before he charges

**Phase 2: The Sprint**
- 40-meter sprint through tunnels
- Players must vault over debris (crouch-jump)
- Heavy item carriers move slower (drop or get caught)

**Phase 3: The Split**
- Tunnel forks into two paths
- **Left path:** Short but has a locked door (needs Green Keycard)
- **Right path:** Longer but open
- Team must decide: split up or all go long way?

**Phase 4: The Trap**
- If team went left (locked door route):
  - One player unlocks door while others hold Long Man at a choke point
  - Guard can barricade briefly, Journalist can stun once
- If team went right (long route):
  - Long Man takes shortcut through wall vent (cuts them off at halfway point)
  - Team must backtrack or fight through

**Phase 5: The Safe Room**
- Locked maintenance closet (Engineer hacks in 3 seconds)
- Everyone piles inside
- Long Man pounds on door for 15 seconds, then leaves
- **First breather moment** — players catch breath, share ammo, plan next move

**Time added:** 5 minutes total for tunnels expansion

## ROOM 5: ELEVATOR ROOM — EXPANSION (+5 minutes)

### Current:
- Find 2 fuses
- Repair generator
- Dual key consoles

### NEW ADDITION:

#### **Auxiliary Power Subpuzzle (5 minutes)**
**Problem:** Dual consoles activate, but screen shows:  
*"AUXILIARY POWER REQUIRED. DORM WING OFFLINE."*

**Solution:**
1. **Find the Dorm Wing** (new hallway off elevator room, leads to abandoned crew quarters)
2. **Dorm Layout:**
   - 4 bunk rooms (search for supplies)
   - Central breaker panel (covered in rust)
   - Locker with **personal item (Son):** Parent's bunk assignment slip

3. **Breaker Puzzle:**
   - Panel shows 6 switches, only 3 are correct
   - **Wiring diagram** is on a desk in one bunk room
   - Engineer must match diagram to panel (trial and error = sparks + Long Man attracted)
   - Correct sequence: `[2-ON, 4-ON, 5-OFF, 1-ON, 3-OFF, 6-ON]`

4. **Complication:** 
   - Long Man spawns in dorm hallway when breakers flip
   - Team must sprint back to elevator room
   - One player stays to hold door shut (Guard) while others reach dual consoles

**Adds:** Exploration, code-breaking, sustained tension, Guard clutch moment

**Time added:** 5 minutes

## ROOM 7: OBSERVATION DECK — NEW ROOM (+8-12 minutes)

### **Location:**
Between Lab Wing and Elevator. The elevator refuses to descend:  
*"OBSERVATION CLEARANCE REQUIRED."*

### **Layout:**
- **Observation Theater:** Rows of chairs facing a two-way mirror
- **Test Chamber:** Behind glass, where Subject 7 was monitored
- **Control Booth:** Above theater, recording equipment and PA system
- **档案室 (Records Room):** Filing cabinets with experiment logs

### **The Final Test Puzzle**

**Setup:**
- PA system crackles to life: *"Observation protocol initiated. Subjects will demonstrate compliance."*
- **4 test stations activate** (one per character type):
  1. **Strength Test** (Guard) — Move 200kg crate onto pressure plate
  2. **Technical Test** (Engineer) — Rewire circuit board in 60 seconds
  3. **Sensory Test** (Son) — Identify which of 4 containers holds a "specimen" (use Sense on jars)
  4. **Documentation Test** (Journalist) — Photograph 3 specific evidence markers in correct order

**Twist:** Tests must be completed **simultaneously** (30-second timer starts when first one begins)

**If failed:** Doors lock, gas vents, team has 10 seconds to restart  
**If succeeded:** Elevator override code appears on observation screen

---

### **The Reveal**
**As team leaves observation deck:**
- Two-way mirror shatters
- Inside test chamber: **Subject 7's empty restraint chair**
- Straps torn, footprints leading to a vent
- **Journal entry unlocks:** "Subject 7 — Status: UNCONTAINED"

**Long Man Final Chase:**
- Spawns in observation deck
- Team must sprint to elevator
- Dual consoles already active (from earlier puzzle)
- Both keys turned → elevator gate closes → Long Man SLAMS into it as doors shut
- **Chapter 1 climax**

**Time added:** 8-12 minutes (exploration + test puzzle + finale chase)
