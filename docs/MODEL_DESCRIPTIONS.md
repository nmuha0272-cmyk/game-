# MODEL_DESCRIPTIONS.md — "Subject Zero" Art Guide

Use these descriptions when building models in Blender, MakeHuman, or Blockbench,
or paste the **AI Prompt** lines into a 3D generator like Meshy or Tripo.

---

## Timeline (so the clothes and gear make sense)

- **1960s:** Project IRONWOOD begins at Site 12 during the Cold War.
  Subject 7 (the Long Man) is one of the first volunteers.
- **1991:** The Cold War ends and IRONWOOD is officially shut down, but a
  secret branch, Program Zero, keeps running.
- **1994:** The Son is born.
- **1999:** The Son, age 5, mails a crayon drawing to his parent at Site 12.
  Months later, the experiments go wrong and Site 12 is sealed with people
  inside. Frank, age 20, is the guard on duty.
- **2015:** The game takes place.

**Look of the game:** The facility never upgraded its Cold War equipment, so
everything inside looks like the 1960s–70s. The four characters wear modern
2015 clothing and carry modern gear. That contrast is part of the creepiness.

## Overall Style

- Grounded and realistic proportions for the humans, slightly worn and dirty.
- Muted colors: olive green, brown, gray, faded navy, rust.
- Each character has **one strong color or shape** so teammates can recognize
  each other in the dark from a distance.
- Monsters should look **human but wrong**: stretched, twisted, or fused with
  machines. Creepy, not gory.
- For game performance, keep models simple (low to mid detail). A PSX-style
  low-poly look is a great option and easier to make.

## The Four Characters

### The Son
- **Age:** 21
- **Build:** Average height, lean, a little restless and jumpy
- **Face:** Messy dark hair, young face with light stubble, serious worried eyes
- **Clothing:** Worn brown canvas jacket over a gray hooded sweatshirt,
  jeans, worn sneakers, a small backpack
- **Recognizable detail:** A hood he often wears up; the jacket's brown color
- **Held item (first-person):** His parent's military dog tags on a chain,
  wrapped around his hand
- **AI Prompt:** *"2015 young man age 21, lean, messy dark hair, light stubble, worn
  brown canvas jacket over gray hoodie, jeans, sneakers, small backpack,
  realistic game character, full body, T-pose"*

### The Journalist
- **Age:** About 35
- **Build:** Medium height, athletic, confident posture
- **Face:** Shoulder-length hair tied back, sharp focused expression
- **Clothing:** Long tan trench coat, dark turtleneck, dark pants, boots,
  a shoulder bag full of notebooks, spare batteries, and memory cards
- **Recognizable detail:** The tan trench coat, visible even in darkness
- **Held item (first-person):** A digital camera (DSLR) with a large external
  flash on top and a neck strap
- **AI Prompt:** *"modern woman investigative journalist around 35, hair tied
  back, long tan trench coat, dark turtleneck, dark pants, boots, shoulder bag,
  DSLR camera with flash on strap, realistic game character, full body, T-pose"*

### The Engineer
- **Age:** About 60
- **Build:** Short, stocky, slightly hunched
- **Face:** Gray thinning hair, thick glasses, deep wrinkles, nervous expression
- **Clothing:** Faded navy blue work coveralls with a name patch (name torn
  off), a heavy tool belt, work boots
- **Recognizable detail:** Navy coveralls and the bulky tool belt
- **Held item (first-person):** A large, scratched steel wrench
- **AI Prompt:** *"older man engineer around 60, stocky, gray thinning hair,
  thick glasses, faded navy blue work coveralls, heavy tool belt, work boots,
  modern day, realistic game character, full body, T-pose"*

### The Guard — Frank Doyle
- **Age:** About 35
- **Build:** Tall, broad shoulders, big and strong
- **Face:** Buzz-cut dark hair, square jaw, a scar through one eyebrow,
  hard tired eyes
- **Clothing:** Old olive green military field jacket with the patches removed,
  plain T-shirt, cargo pants, heavy combat boots
- **Recognizable detail:** His large size and the olive green jacket
- **Held item (first-person):** A heavy metal police-style flashlight
- **AI Prompt:** *"tall muscular man around 35, buzz cut dark hair, square
  jaw, scar through eyebrow, faded olive green military field jacket, cargo
  pants, combat boots, former soldier, modern day, realistic game character,
  full body, T-pose"*

## The Failed Experiments

### Subject 7 — "The Long Man" (Chapter 1)
- **Size:** About 9 feet tall when standing, but usually crawls on all fours
- **Body:** Human shape stretched too far. Extremely long thin arms and legs,
  long fingers, a spine that curves because he's always hunched over
- **Face:** Pale, sunken cheeks, squinting eyes that hate light
- **Clothing:** A torn, too-small hospital gown and the remains of a
  military ID bracelet on his wrist
- **Movement:** Crawls through tunnels, reaches his arms through vents and
  under doors, covers his eyes when flashed
- **AI Prompt:** *"extremely tall thin humanoid creature, very long arms and
  legs, long fingers, hunched curved spine, pale skin, sunken face, torn
  hospital gown, horror game monster, not gory, full body"*

### "The Wired" (Chapter 2)
- **Size:** Normal human height
- **Body:** A thin man with thick old electrical cables growing out of his back
  and arms, some still plugged into the walls. Small lights on the cables
  flicker when he moves
- **Face:** Metal headpiece with wires bolted to it, eyes glowing faintly
- **Clothing:** Remains of a lab test subject uniform, gray and scorched
- **Movement:** Twitchy and jerky; moves faster when the power is on
- **AI Prompt:** *"thin humanoid creature with thick old electrical cables
  growing from its back and arms, retro metal headpiece with wires, faintly
  glowing eyes, scorched gray uniform, 1960s machinery, horror game monster,
  not gory, full body"*

### "The Knockers" (Chapter 3)
- **Size:** Normal human height, several of them
- **Body:** Very thin, gray-pale skin, long overgrown hair and nails
- **Face:** Eyes covered by cloudy film (they are blind), mouths always slightly open
- **Clothing:** Faded orange-tan 1960s facility jumpsuits with numbers on the chest
- **Movement:** Shuffle slowly, tilt their heads toward sounds, then rush
  suddenly when they hear something
- **Variation:** Make one base model, then change the hair, jumpsuit number,
  and posture so the group looks different
- **AI Prompt:** *"very thin pale humanoid, blind cloudy eyes, long overgrown
  hair, faded 1960s numbered prisoner jumpsuit, hunched posture, horror game
  monster, not gory, full body"*

### Subject Zero (Chapter 4)
- **Size:** Slightly taller than a normal person
- **Body:** Almost perfectly human, which is what makes it scary. Too still,
  too calm, with skin that's a little too smooth
- **Face:** Calm, blank expression. Resembles the Son a little (it may be his parent)
- **Clothing:** A clean white 1960s test subject uniform with "0" on the
  chest, strangely untouched after all these years
- **Movement:** Walks slowly and never runs. Tilts its head. Copies voices
- **AI Prompt:** *"tall calm humanoid figure, almost human but unnaturally
  smooth skin, blank calm expression, clean white 1960s test subject uniform
  with the number 0 on the chest, eerie, horror game character, full body"*
