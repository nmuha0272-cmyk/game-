# Phase 11 — Playtest Checklist for Chapter 1: "The Surface"

## How to Use
Run Chapter 1 with 2-4 players. After each session, mark observations 
with ✅ (works), ❌ (doesn't work), ❓ (unsure), or ⚠️ (issue noted). 
Redesign any section where all players could finish alone.

---

## Core Loop Observations

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Core Loop: Explore → Find Gear → Solve Puzzles → Survive → Uncover Story**
- Does the game flow naturally from one phase to the next?
- Are players motivated to explore and share gear?
- Is there a clear sense of progression?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Chapter 1 Introduction**
- Do players understand their character's motivation after the intro?
- Is the lore (tapes, files, photos) understandable?
- Is the transition from the gate to the building clear?

## Teamwork Checks

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **No Solo Solutions**
- Can any single player finish any room alone?
- Which rooms feel like they require 2+ players?
- Which rooms feel too easy with fewer players?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Everyone Matters**
- Does each character ability get used at least once?
- **Eli (Son):** Sense ability — highlighted monsters?
- **Cole (Guard):** Hold doors, move heavy objects?
- **Nora (Journalist):** Camera flash stun, photo evidence?
- **Walter (Engineer):** Fix generators, hack panels?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Split the Team**
- Are there sections where players are in different rooms?
- Do players need to describe what they see to each other?
- Are there information gaps that require talking?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Cover Each Other**
- Are there timed tasks that leave a player vulnerable?
- Are there heavy items that need carrying?
- Is the downed/revoke system (30 seconds) clear?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Share Gear**
- Is the inventory system (4 slots + held item) intuitive?
- Do players pass items to each other naturally?
- Are pouch upgrades found and used?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Reward Communication**
- Are there information-only-visible-to-one-player moments?
- Do players need to describe codes, maps, or clues to each other?

---

## Multiplayer Observations

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Network Sync**
- Do all players see the same events (gate opening, elevator snap)?
- Is the lobby/arrival consistent across host and clients?
- Are lore items collectible by all players?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Spawn Positions**
- Do spawns work for 2 players? 3 players? 4 players?
- Are spawn points too close, causing overlap?
- Is the porch area tight but functional?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Item Handoff**
- Can items be dropped and picked up by other players?
- Is the "give item" mechanic clear?
- Are heavy items handled correctly?

---

## Accessibility & Polish

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **UI/UX**
- Is the journal menu (Files/Tapes/Photos/Personal) understandable?
- Are lore entries clear and readable?
- Is the flashlight battery system clear?
- Is the noise meter (if present) understandable?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Audio/Visual**
- Is the rain ambient sound consistent?
- Are monster sounds clear and directional?
- Are music stingers at appropriate moments?
- Are sound cues for monster hearing clear?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Performance**
- Does the game run at acceptable FPS on target hardware?
- Are there any frame drops during monster chases?
- Is the particle effects performance acceptable (flies, rain, etc.)?

---

## Balance & Tuning

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Monster Speed**
- Is Goliath fast enough to be threatening but not unfair?
- Does the monster catch the "right" player (the one with most noise/items)?
- Can players hide effectively?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Ability Cooldowns**
- **Eli Sense:** 20s cooldown — too long, too short?
- **Cole Strength:** Does holding doors work reliably?
- **Nora Flash:** 15s cooldown — does stun work on Goliath?
- **Walter Repair:** Does fixing generators feel rewarding?

### [✅/❌/❓/⚠️] **Resource Management**
- **Ammo scarcity:** Is it too plentiful or too scarce?
- **Battery drain:** Do players understand battery consumption?
- **Inventory slots:** 4 slots + held item — too few, enough?
