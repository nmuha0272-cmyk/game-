VOICE CLIPS FOR THE OPENING
===========================
Put a recording here named after its line ID and it plays with that line
(the subtitle stays up as long as the clip). .ogg, .wav or .mp3 all work.

  road_01.ogg ... road_14.ogg   Cutscene 1 "The Road" (Eli and Cole in the car)
  meet_01.ogg ... meet_08.ogg   Cutscene 2 "The Meeting" (the gas station)

The words for every line are in data/opening_dialogue.json. Change the text
there (no code needed); keep the "id" the same as the clip's name.
